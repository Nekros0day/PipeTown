# Mechanics Lab

The `Lab` developer button opens ten authored dynamic-routing experiments outside campaign progress. The suite has been pruned to keep one literal gate, improve the flood and spinner ideas, and replace the rest with mechanics that create visible, understandable consequences instead of random destruction. Use `Prev`, `Next`, or `Continue` after clearing a test to move through the suite.

## Playable Experiments

| # | Mechanic | Player Decision | Why It Can Be Fun |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pump Gate | Supply water to open the only internet crossing. | A readable prerequisite that turns a sealed barrier into progress. |
| 2 | Patch Flood | Drain seeded puddles or race power through temporary gaps. | Flooding is distributed and readable rather than a straight band. |
| 3 | Spinner Spray | Time power around a rotating wet sweep or route wide. | A clear moving hazard creates timing and path-choice pressure. |
| 4 | One-Way Main | Draw with the arrowed service flow, not back against it. | Direction rules add planning without adding more endpoints. |
| 5 | Leak Cracks | Water through cracks creates puddles that power must avoid. | A normal success can visibly change the board for later routes. |
| 6 | Moving Crew | Wait for a crossing cart, weave past it, or detour. | The player reads motion and commits at the right moment. |
| 7 | Shared Trunk | Split one water main into multiple homes. | Reinforces branch planning without a timer or hidden meter. |
| 8 | Signal Storm | Time internet around a drifting interference cloud. | A soft moving obstacle changes safe space over time. |
| 9 | Root Wake | Water wakes roots that spread into gas lanes. | Order matters, but the hazard grows visibly from the player's action. |
| 10 | Fume Split | Gas leaves fumes; heat must go first or keep distance. | Utility interaction creates separation pressure without hidden failure. |

## Interaction Principles

- Hazards are drawn and labelled in the exact passage they affect before a route can fail.
- A setback cancels only the active line and reports why; it does not randomly destroy a home.
- Each non-gate test should ask for a different tactic: timing, route efficiency, route direction, order planning, or utility separation.
- Flood and growth mechanics should use seeded distributed cells or organic clusters rather than flat straight-line walls.

## Campaign Rollout Rules

- Pump Gate and Fume Split now have guarded campaign hooks. They are selected only after a generated board has a retained legal witness solution, and the generator rejects the mechanic if the witness does not prove its extra rule.
- Other Mechanics Lab behavior is still experimental only; generated campaign levels do not apply those transitions yet.
- A new mechanic can enter procedural generation only after the generator represents its state transitions, retains a legal witness solution, and audits deterministic solvability.
- Timing mechanics need a non-timed alternative or a generous retry path before appearing in normal progression.
- Clean-run stars are a suitable consequence for mistakes; permanent random destruction is not.
